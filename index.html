Our Brand Story ✨

Crafted with Love By Kainat Abdullah 🌿

Homemade, Pure, and 100% Hygienic 🧼

Luvo Organic was born out of a simple, uncompromising belief: skincare should be pure, intentional, and deeply nourishing 🍃. Every single soap bar is lovingly made at home in small batches by Kainat Abdullah to ensure complete purity and warmth in every product 🌸.

In a world full of harsh chemical additives and synthetic formulas, we offer an authentic, natural alternative 🌿. We carefully blend botanical ingredients, rich essential oils, and raw elements that respect your skin's natural balance ✨.

Why Luvo Organic? 💚

  • ✨ 100% Handcrafted: Prepared with care in hygienic batches for fresh, organic potency.
  • 🌱 Sustainably Sourced: Ethical botanicals that are gentle on your skin and kind to nature.
  • 🚫 Zero Compromises: Totally free from harsh sulfates, artificial fillers, and toxic chemicals.
